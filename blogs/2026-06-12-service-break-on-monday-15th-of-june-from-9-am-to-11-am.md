On Monday, June 15, starting at 9 AM, the platform will undergo a major WordPress update to version 7.0. During the same maintenance window, the Consent Management Platform will be changed from Cookiebot to Usercentrics. The service may be partially or completely unavailable during the maintenance, and we do not recommend updating sites during this time.
