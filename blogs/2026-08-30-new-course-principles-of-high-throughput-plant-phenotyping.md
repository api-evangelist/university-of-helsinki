New course: Principles of High-Throughput Plant Phenotyping chenghao@helsinki.fi Sun, 30/08/2026 - 11:30 PheNo – the Norwegian Plant Phenotyping Infrastructure – is offering a new course, BIO412 – Principles of High-Throughput Plant Phenotyping (5 ECTS), running 22 September–9 October 2026 at NMBU, UiO and online. Applications are now open. This course provides a practical and research-oriented introduction to modern high-throughput plant phenotyping.
