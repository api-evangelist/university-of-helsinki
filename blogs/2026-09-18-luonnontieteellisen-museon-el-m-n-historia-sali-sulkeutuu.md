Natural History Museum’s History of Life exhibition to close early next year – new exhibition to open in late 2028 johanemi@helsinki.fi Wed, 23/09/2026 - 13:51 A new exhibition exploring the origins of life and the development of biodiversity will replace the History of Life exhibition. The new exhibition is scheduled to open in late 2028, and the timetable for the construction work is currently being planned. The Natural History Museum’s History of Life is one of the museum’s most popular exhibitions.
