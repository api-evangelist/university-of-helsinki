Creative Destruction – A photographic series by Johan Eichhorn johanemi@helsinki.fi Tue, 28/07/2026 - 12:25 The exhibition is on view at the Finnish Museum of Natural History’s PopUp balcony from 1 September 2026 to 10 January 2027. Johan Eichhorn’s Creative Destruction shifts human interventions in nature into the human living environment. Instead of forest roads cutting through woodlands, drainage drying out wetlands, and dams altering waterways, these elements appear in people’s homes and other everyday spaces.
