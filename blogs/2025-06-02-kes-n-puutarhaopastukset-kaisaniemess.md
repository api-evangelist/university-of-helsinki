Garden Highlights – Free guided tours in Kaisaniemi Botanic Garden johanemi@helsinki.fi Thu, 11/06/2026 - 15:05 Free guided tours will be held at the Kaisaniemi Botanic Garden from June 23 to August 22, 2026 On a guided tour, you’ll journey through millions of years of evolution along the paths of the Evolution Garden, and immerse yourself in a world of scents and colours in the Garden of the senses.
