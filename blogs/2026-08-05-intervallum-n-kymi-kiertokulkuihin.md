Intervallum – Views on cycles johanemi@helsinki.fi Wed, 05/08/2026 - 08:26 Emmu Johansson’s exhibition is on display at the Gallery Corridor of the Kaisaniemi Greenhouses from 26 August to 11 October 2026. Emmu Johanssons exhibition Intervallum covers junctions of cycles and systems in repetitiveness. She is a painter whose works have been presented in Finnish Painters' Unions Gallery and Sinebrychoff Art Museum.
