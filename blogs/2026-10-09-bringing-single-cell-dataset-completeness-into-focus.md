Bringing single-cell dataset completeness into focus palmroos@helsinki.fi Fri, 09/10/2026 - 10:19 Researchers at FIMM have developed a computational framework for estimating cellular diversity in single-cell datasets. The method provides a new way to assess the completeness of single-cell studies and addresses a longstanding challenge in understanding how much of a tissue's cellular diversity has been captured. Over the past decade, single-cell sequencing has revolutionized biological research, enabling scientists to investigate tissues at unprecedented resolution.
