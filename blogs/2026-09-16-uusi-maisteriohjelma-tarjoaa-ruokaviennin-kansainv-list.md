New master’s programme builds international expertise in food export heheiki@helsinki.fi Thu, 17/09/2026 - 11:34 The University of Helsinki will soon offer studies combining international business with expert knowledge of the food sector. Despite high demand, professionals with this skill set remain scarce in Finland. The English-language Master’s Programme in International Food Business and Gastronomy equips students with relevant skills in branding, export, gastronomy and sustainability.
