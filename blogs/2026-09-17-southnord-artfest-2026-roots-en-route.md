Southnord Artfest 2026: Roots En Route johanemi@helsinki.fi Thu, 17/09/2026 - 13:45 Southnord Artfest festival unfolds across the greenhouses of the Kaisaniemi Botanic Garden 17.10.2026 – 10.1.2027. Roots En Route is a multi-sensory installation by four artists who will create a multi-sensory installation in dialogue with the greenhouses – examining rootedness and displacement; asking what grows where it was planted and what survives the crossing. The African plants that fill the greenhouses carry their own histories of movement and adaptation.
