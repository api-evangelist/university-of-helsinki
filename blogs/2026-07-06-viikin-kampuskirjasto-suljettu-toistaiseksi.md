Viikki Campus Library closed until further notice vristika@helsinki.fi Mon, 06/07/2026 - 10:38 Water damage that occurred on Saturday, 4th of July, has forced the closure of the Viikki Campus Library until further notice to allow for drying and repair work. You can make requests for Viikki’s collections through Helka and the pick-up the books in the Kaisa House. The Viikki Campus Library will be closed until at least August 2, 2026 due to drying and repair work caused by water damage.
