Jenny and Antti Wihuri Foundation award a grant for a joint university museum project vlkpehko@helsinki.fi Fri, 16/10/2026 - 09:07 The exhibition- and event-based project on controversies in the history of scholarship will be carried out in Helsinki and Jyväskylä from 2027 to 2028. The Jenny and Antti Wihuri Foundation has awarded €50,000 to Helsinki University Museum Flame and Jyväskylä University Museum for a collaborative project focused on exhibitions and events. This award is a significant boon for the project.
