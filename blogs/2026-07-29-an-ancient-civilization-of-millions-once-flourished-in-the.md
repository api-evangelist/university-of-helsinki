An ancient civilization of millions once flourished in the Amazon merimaaj@helsinki.fi Wed, 29/07/2026 - 16:38 For nearly 1,500 years, the Aquiry civilization shaped southwestern Amazonia, and new research reveals it was far more populous than previously thought. Aerial laser scanning uncovered hundreds of ceremonial centers and the road systems. The civilization's impact is still visible in the Amazon's vegetation today.
