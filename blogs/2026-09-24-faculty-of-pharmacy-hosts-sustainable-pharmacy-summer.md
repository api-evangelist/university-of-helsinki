Faculty of Pharmacy hosts Sustainable Pharmacy Summer School for the first time in Finland lindymar@helsinki.fi Thu, 24/09/2026 - 12:40 The event attracted 76 participants from 16 countries to register for the 5th Summer School on Sustainable Pharmacy, a four-day event held on 7–10 September 2026 at the Viikki Campus of University of Helsinki. The summer school has previously taken place at the University of Freiburg (2022 and 2024), Kiel University (2023), and Martin Luther University Halle-Wittenberg (2025). Participants included representatives from academia, agencies, the healthcare sector
