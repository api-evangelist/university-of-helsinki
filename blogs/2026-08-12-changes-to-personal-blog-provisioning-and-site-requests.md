The Blogs.helsinki.fi service will introduce two changes related to blog provisioning starting on 24 August 2026 at 10:00 AM. Personal Blogs After the date and time above, personal blogs will no longer be created automatically for new users. As university account identifiers have changed over time, automatically generated blog addresses are no longer considered practical or user-friendly.
