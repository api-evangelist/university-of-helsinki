Bridging the divide: UniverCities 2026 connected research and city life litili@helsinki.fi Thu, 08/10/2026 - 16:32 Helsinki Institute of Urban and Regional Studies (Urbaria) had the joy of hosting the second edition of the UniverCities conference on 29-30 September. UniverCities 2026 brought together over 100 participants from 20 countries, with 47 presentations, 3 keynotes and 2 panel discussions. Urban research is stronger than ever at the University of Helsinki and in the capital region.
