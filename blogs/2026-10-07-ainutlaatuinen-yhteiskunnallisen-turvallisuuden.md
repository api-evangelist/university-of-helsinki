Unique Master's Programme in Societal Security launches in autumn 2027 – applications open in January pakomula@helsinki.fi Thu, 08/10/2026 - 14:03 The Faculty of Social Sciences' new Master's Programme in Societal Security is aimed at students from diverse academic backgrounds in the social sciences and history. Applications to the programme will open in January 2027 in the joint application. English will be the primary language of instruction.
