"Without pollinators, the world would be bleak and colorless" – A new exhibition opens the door to the lives of pollinators johanemi@helsinki.fi Wed, 22/04/2026 - 14:42 The world of pollinators comes to life in the new Pollinator Diversity exhibition, opening at the Natural History Museum on May 20, 2026. Pollinators represent an astonishingly diverse group of animals, many of which possess remarkable abilities despite their small size. Bees can count, bumblebees solve problems, and on distant islands, even lizards can pollinate flowers.
