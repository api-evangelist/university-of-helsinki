New Nordic project launches to strengthen knowledge on intimate partner homicide inkajarv@helsinki.fi Fri, 16/10/2026 - 11:13 A new Nordic research project coordinated by KRIMO has been launched to strengthen knowledge and improve the monitoring of intimate partner homicide across the Nordic countries. Developing EHM-based Intimate partner homicide monitoring and research system in the Nordic Area , funded by the Nordic Council of Ministers, brings together researchers and experts from Finland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Iceland, the Netherlands and Switzerland.
