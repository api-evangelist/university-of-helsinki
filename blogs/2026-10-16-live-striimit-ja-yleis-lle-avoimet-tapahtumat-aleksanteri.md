Streams and events open to the public in the Aleksanteri Conference 2026 turmaija@helsinki.fi Fri, 16/10/2026 - 10:12 In the Aleksanteri Conference of 2026, there are sessions and plenaries open to the public without a fee. Welcome! The 25th Aleksanteri Conference, entitled “Mapping Security in Central and Eastern Europe and Eurasia: Fault Lines and Frontlines,” brings together the latest research on security and focuses particularly on Eurasia as well as Eastern and Central Europe.The conference programme is partly open to the public and available via live stream.
