Donate: AI should be taught sholttin@helsinki.fi Thu, 01/10/2026 - 14:27 Artificial intelligence is transforming learning, and so we need new knowledge and skills. This way, the more than a million children and adolescents in Finland will receive the education they deserve – research-based, equal and forward-looking.
