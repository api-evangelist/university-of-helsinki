Sharing our research at 20th Annual FinPharmaNet meeting durancri@helsinki.fi Fri, 21/08/2026 - 16:01 A great opportunity to share our research, exchange ideas, connect with colleagues from across Finland — and bring home a prize! brought together doctoral researchers and the wider drug research community from across Finland on 20–21 August in Rauhalahti, Kuopio. The two-day meeting was a great chance to discover what other pharmacy units are working on, share ideas and, of course, meet fellow researchers from around the country.
