Finnish Teacher Education Forum celebrated its 10 years anniversary kakleemo@helsinki.fi Thu, 27/08/2026 - 16:08 The Finnish Teacher Education Forum has been operating for a decade and in August it gathered in Tampere University Faculty of Education and Culture for the Anniversary Seminar. Teacher Education Forum has made a variety of achievements during its existence. Visioning the future of the Finnish teacher education and concretizing the plans in broad collaboration is an established way of working together in the Forum.
