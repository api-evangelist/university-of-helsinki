Kasvu Therapeutics develops a novel way to treat depression ksalmiva@helsinki.fi Thu, 24/09/2026 - 09:00 Kasvu Therapeutics, a University of Helsinki spinout, raised €30 million to develop a novel depression drug. The company arose from the University’s breakthrough research. The drug development company Kasvu Therapeutics (Kasvu) put out announcing it has raised €30 million to develop pharmaceuticals for the treatment of major depressive disorder and other brain diseases.
