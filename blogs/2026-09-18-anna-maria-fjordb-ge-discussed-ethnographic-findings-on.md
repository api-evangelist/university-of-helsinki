Anna Maria Fjordbøge discussed ethnographic findings on digitalisation and judicial independence inkajarv@helsinki.fi Fri, 18/09/2026 - 09:58 On 17 September, Krimo hosted a Brown Bag Seminar featuring Anna Maria Fjordbøge from the University of Copenhagen, who is currently visiting Krimo throughout September. In her presentation, “Blurring boundaries? Negotiating digitalization, efficiency efforts and tripartition of power at the Danish courts” Fjordbøge discussed the ongoing digitalisation of the Danish courts and the penal chain.
